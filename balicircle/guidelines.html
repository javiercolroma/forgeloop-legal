Community guidelines

Last updated: 25 October 2026

We want a safe, welcoming community. By using Bali Circle you accept these guidelines. We apply zero tolerance to objectionable content and abusive members.

Forbidden content

  • Harassment, bullying or threats towards other people.
  • Hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, orientation, disability, etc.
  • Nudity, sexual or sexually suggestive content.
  • Violence, self-harm or content that promotes dangerous substances.
  • Illegal content, spam, scams, commercial solicitation or impersonation.
  • Material that infringes copyright or the rights of others.

How we keep the community safe

  • Report: on any profile or message, open the ⋯ menu and tap "Report".
  • Block: from the same menu you can block a member; they won't see you and you won't see them, and they can't message you.
  • Moderation: we review reports, remove infringing content and ban abusive members within 24 hours at most.

Consequences

Breaking these guidelines may lead to removal of content, limited features or deletion of the account.

Report a problem or appeal
