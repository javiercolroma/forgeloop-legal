Bali Circle

Last updated: 25 October 2026

A private social club for active people in Bali. Available on TestFlight and soon on the App Store.

Questions, problems or feedback about Bali Circle? Write to soporte@forgeloop.app and we'll get back to you within 48 hours.

Common questions

  • Why does the app need my location? Bali Circle only works in Bali. Your location confirms you're here and shows how far people are. Others only see an approximate distance, never where you are.
  • I'm not in Bali yet. You can create your profile now; your circle opens when you land. If you have an access code, enter it on that screen.
  • How do I hide that I'm online? Settings → Your Circle → turn off "Show when I'm online" and/or "Show my distance".
  • How do I block or report someone? Open their profile or the chat and use the ⋯ menu.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. Everything is removed permanently.
