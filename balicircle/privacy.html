Bali Circle

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Bali Circle is a private social club for active people in Bali. This policy explains what we process and why.

Data we process

  • Account: your name and the email from Sign in with Apple (it may be a private relay address).
  • Profile: photos and videos, age, where you're from, your area in Bali, how long you're staying, your sports, bio and what you're looking for.
  • Location, only while the app is open. It is rounded to a grid of about 110 m before it is stored. Other members only ever see an approximate distance ("1.2 km"), never your position. It is also used to confirm you are in Bali.
  • Activity status: whether you're online, if you choose to show it. You can hide your distance and your online status in Settings.
  • Messages with other members: text, photos, videos, voice notes and locations you choose to share.
  • Moments you share. They disappear after 24 hours unless you keep them on your profile.
  • Reports and blocks, to keep the community safe.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't rate people's looks or use your photos to train models.

Where it is stored

On our servers, hosted by Supabase (EU region). Media files are stored in private buckets served through public links that are hard to guess.

Your rights

  • Edit your data at any time from your profile.
  • Delete your account and all its data from Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, photos, moments, messages and location data permanently.
  • Ask us anything about your data at soporte@forgeloop.app.

Age

Bali Circle is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use dating features.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll let you know about relevant changes inside the app.
